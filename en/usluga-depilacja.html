Waxing in Szczecin

We wax single areas and packages — the bikini, legs and underarms set is the most popular, since one visit covers you for several weeks. Wax removes the hair with the root, so regrowth is finer and comes back later than after shaving.

For the wax to grip, the hair needs to be about five millimetres long — roughly two weeks since the last shave. Hair that is too short cannot be removed and the appointment has to be moved, so put the razor away beforehand.

For 24 hours after waxing skip the sauna, pool and solarium, and avoid strong scrubs for two days. Regular exfoliation between visits reduces ingrown hairs — start three or four days after the appointment.
