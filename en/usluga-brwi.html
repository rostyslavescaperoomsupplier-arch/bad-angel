Brows and lash lamination in Szczecin

Brow lamination sets the hairs in one direction and holds that shape for four to six weeks. It suits people whose brows grow in different directions or have gaps — once set, the arch looks fuller without pencil. We add botox to the lamination to rebuild the hair after the chemical step.

We set brow geometry to your features, not to one trending shape. We shape with wax and tweezers and tint with powder henna, which holds on the skin for a few days and on the hair for up to three weeks, so the arch stays filled even where hairs are missing.

Lash lamination is an alternative to extensions: your own lashes are lifted at the root and curled, with nothing glued on. The effect holds for six to eight weeks and needs no fills — a good option if you want an open look without returning every three weeks.
