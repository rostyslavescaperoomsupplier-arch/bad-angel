Massage in Szczecin — types and duration

The most requested treatment is a back massage — half an hour on the neck and thoracic spine, after which turning your head is easier again. It suits desk workers, drivers and anyone whose shoulders go numb by the end of the day. A full-body classic massage takes an hour and a half, a relaxing one two hours.

We also offer honey, anti-cellulite, Chinese cupping and facial massage. Honey and cupping work harder and can leave redness for a few hours — we say so beforehand, so you do not book them the day before wearing a backless dress.

Prices differ for women and men because the time and workload over a larger body area differ — both rates are listed above. Before a first massage we ask about injuries, pregnancy, varicose veins and chronic conditions; some of them require a doctor's clearance.
