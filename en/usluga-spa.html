Hand SPA and paraffin in Szczecin

A paraffin treatment is a warm wrap that seals moisture into the skin and warms the joints of the hand. In winter, when skin cracks from cold and dry office air, it is the simplest way to stop your hands stinging within a day or two.

A hand SPA combines a scrub, a mask and a massage — half an hour, and it pairs well with a manicure in the same visit. The same treatment is available for feet, usually added to a pedicure before summer.

The treatment is safe for most people, but we do not use paraffin on fresh cuts, rashes or where sensation is impaired. Tell us beforehand if you have circulation problems.
