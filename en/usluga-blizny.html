Scar and stretch mark reduction in Szczecin — microneedling

Microneedling punctures the skin with fine needles to a controlled depth. The damage is deliberate and microscopic: it triggers collagen rebuilding, so the scar flattens and fades and the stretch mark loses its sharp edge. The result builds over several weeks after the session, not immediately.

The consultation is free and always precedes the first session. We look at the scar, ask when it formed and how it healed, and tell you how many sessions are realistically needed — fresh scars respond faster than decade-old ones, and keloids need a different approach. We do not start without that conversation.

We work separately with caesarean scars, old post-surgical scars and stretch marks on the bust, abdomen, buttocks and thighs. The skin stays red for a day or two afterwards, so avoid booking it right before an important occasion.
