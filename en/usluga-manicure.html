Manicure in Szczecin — what to know

Clients come to our salon at aleja Wyzwolenia 5/10 from across Szczecin — the city centre, Śródmieście, Turzyn and Niebuszewo. Every appointment starts with an assessment of the nail plate and cuticles, because that decides whether the gel polish lasts three weeks or chips after a few days. We work with a combined technique: an e-file for the nail fold, scissors wherever precision matters.

The price list covers hygienic, hybrid, Japanese, men's and dry-technique manicure, plus gel nails and dual-form extensions in several lengths. We advise on length and shape based on your hand and your daily routine: short almonds work well if you type all day, longer squares suit special occasions. Colour is chosen on the spot from a full swatch set.

You can book online through Booksy at any hour. Allow extra time for a first visit: if the nail plate needs repair, or you are having work from another salon filled in, the appointment runs longer. If you are unsure which service to pick, message us and we will match it to the price list and to a realistic appointment length.
